Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Stride ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Stride mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we collect:

  • Your name and email address (provided by your OAuth provider)
  • A unique user ID associated with your account

When you use the app, we store:

  • Goals, habits, tasks, and milestones you create
  • XP points and progress data
  • Notification preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To sync your goals, habits, and tasks across sessions
  • To display your name and progress within the app
  • To send local push notifications you have enabled

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is protected with row-level security and encrypted in transit using TLS.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to operate the services described above (e.g., Supabase for data storage, Google/Apple for authentication).

5. Children's Privacy

Stride is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
