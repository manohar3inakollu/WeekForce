Delete Your Stride Account

Last updated: May 14, 2026

You can request deletion of your Stride account and all associated data at any time by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

Step 1 — Send an email Email us at inakollu.manohar@gmail.com with the subject line: "Delete My Stride Account"
Step 2 — Include your account email Include the email address you used to sign in to Stride (Google or Apple account email).
Step 3 — Confirmation We will confirm your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Email us to delete account

What data is deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your name and email address
  • All goals, milestones, habits, and tasks you created
  • Your XP points and progress history
  • Your notification preferences and settings

No data is retained after deletion. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. All your goals, habits, and progress will be lost and cannot be recovered.
